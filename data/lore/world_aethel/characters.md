# Notable Characters of Aethel

This document records the backgrounds and traits of the characters that the author has written so far. It describes who they are, not where they are or what they carry at any moment.

## Aria Voss

Aria Voss was born in a fishing village on the Northern Reach and came to the Ice Citadel at the age of twelve. She trained under the Frost Wardens and became a flint-bearer, one of the few scouts licensed to carry flint out of the Flint Vault. She is an Adept, so her mana pool holds up to 250 mana, and she can cast in both the Fire and the Frost disciplines.

Aria is patient, dry and stubborn. She keeps an oath she made as a young scout, never to leave a Warden behind in the snow. She distrusts the Ember Court and has never been to Ember Keep. Her old teacher says that she is the best tracker the Citadel has trained in thirty years, and the worst at following orders she does not agree with.

## Brannoc Hale

Brannoc Hale was born in Ember Keep, the son of a smith. He is mana-blind, which means that he has no pool and cannot cast, and that no charm or scrying can touch him. This is why the Cinder Guard made him a captain, a post he took at twenty-six.

Brannoc is blunt and loyal. He fights with a war hammer that his father forged from ember iron, and he is known in the Keep for never having lost a duel. He distrusts magic of every kind, including healing, and has refused it more than once. He believes that the Concord of Glass is the only reason that the Keep still stands.

## Sera Quill

Sera Quill is an Archivist of the Glass Library and an Adept of the Aether discipline, with a pool of up to 250 mana. She came to the Library as an orphan and rose quickly through the ranks. She is curious, careful and quiet, and her work is to copy and mend the oldest texts of the Archive.

There is a rumour in the Library that Sera has read the blood-casting treatises in the Restricted Stacks. The official records say that she has never entered the Restricted Stacks, and that she holds no silver key. Sera has never confirmed or denied the rumour, and the Head Archivist has declined to comment on it.
