# The Glass Library

## Overview

The Glass Library stands on the Shard Plateau in the middle of Aethel, halfway between the Ice Citadel and Ember Keep. It was built from the fused glass that the Sundering left behind, so its walls, floors and shelves are all made of clear or smoky glass, and the light inside is cold and bright. It is the home of the Archivists of Glass, the neutral order that keeps the records of the world.

About three hundred Archivists, scribes and students live and work in the Library. It holds the Archive, the only complete record of the laws of magic.

## Rules of the Library

### Magic inside the Library

All magic is silenced inside the Glass Library, except for spells cast by a sworn Archivist. The glass walls absorb every working of every discipline, so a visitor who tries to cast a spell inside the Library simply feels the mana drain away with no effect. A spellbook can be read inside the Library, but nothing can be cast from it.

### The Library is warded

The Glass Library is warded on all sides. Teleportation cannot cross its walls, in either direction. Visitors arrive by the road, and the only way in is through the Door of Quiet on the southern side.

### The Restricted Stacks

The Restricted Stacks are a sealed wing of the Library where the most dangerous texts are kept, including the treatises on blood-casting. The door of the Restricted Stacks can be opened only with a silver key. There are exactly three silver keys. The Head Archivist holds one, the Deputy Archivist holds the second, and the Keeper of the Concord holds the third.

### Borrowing and copying

No book may leave the Library. A visitor who wishes to keep a text must copy it by hand, using the Library's own ink, which is sold at the Door of Quiet in sealed vials. Copies made with any other ink are treated as forgeries and confiscated.

## The Archive of the Concord

The Library keeps the original text of the Concord of Glass, the treaty that ended the War of Embers. The Concord is displayed in a case of clear glass in the central hall, and any visitor may read it for free. The Archivists are the guardians of the Concord and have the right to inspect any caster suspected of blood-casting.

## Travel

The Shard Plateau is two days by foot from the Ash Road. The roads are good, and the Library is the one place in Aethel where travellers from the Ice Citadel and Ember Keep can sit at the same table without a guard.
