# Ember Keep

## Overview

Ember Keep is a fortress-city built into the flank of the volcano Mount Cinder, in the southern Cinderlands. About six thousand people live there. It is the seat of the Ember Court, the forge of the southern kingdoms, and the rival of the Ice Citadel in every way a fortress can be a rival.

The air in the Keep is warm all year and smells of hot iron. Rivers of slow lava are channelled through the lower city to drive the great forges, and the streets of the upper city are paved with black glass.

## Places

### The Cinder Gate

The Cinder Gate is the main entrance of the Keep, a vast door of blackened iron set into the southern rampart. It was the site of the great siege of Year 215. The gate has been rebuilt twice since, and the Keep's soldiers, the Cinder Guard, keep watch on it day and night.

### The Forges

The forges of Ember Keep produce the best iron in Aethel, known as ember iron. Ember iron holds an edge for a lifetime and is the reason the Keep is rich. Smiths in the Keep are licensed by the Ember Court, and a smith who sells ember iron to the Frost Wardens without a licence is exiled.

### The Cinder Guard

The Cinder Guard is the standing army of the Ember Court. It numbers about nine hundred soldiers. Its captains are chosen for their skill with a hammer or a blade, and many of them are mana-blind, because the Ember Court values soldiers who cannot be charmed or scried.

## Rules of the Keep

### Frost magic in Ember Keep

Frost magic costs double mana inside Ember Keep. The heat of the volcano pushes against every working of the Frost discipline, so a frost lance that costs 15 mana anywhere else costs 30 mana inside the Keep. Fire magic is not affected, and it costs the normal price.

### The Keep has no wards

Ember Keep has never been warded. The Ember Court holds that walls are for those who are afraid of their neighbours, and has refused every offer of a ward. A caster can therefore teleport into the Keep, or out of it, for the normal price of 50 mana.

### Carrying flint

Flint is common in the Cinderlands and has no special meaning in the Keep. Any visitor can buy a flint at the market for a copper coin.

## Travel

The Ash Road runs north from the Cinder Gate to the Ice Citadel. A rider on a fast horse can reach the Citadel in five days. Travellers on foot need nine days. The southern road leads to the Salt Marshes and is much quieter.
