# The Magic of Aethel

Magic in Aethel is paid for in mana. Every spell has a price, every caster has a limited pool, and the places of the world bend the rules in their own ways. This document lists the general laws. Individual places add their own rules, which are described in the location documents.

## Mana

Mana is the energy that every spell is paid for with. A caster who cannot pay the full cost of a spell cannot cast it.

### Pool sizes

The size of a caster's mana pool depends on their rank. An Initiate holds up to 100 mana, an Adept holds up to 250 mana, and an Archmage holds up to 500 mana. Rank is awarded by the Archivists of Glass after an examination, and it never changes the cost of a spell, only the number of spells a caster can afford in a day.

### Regeneration

Mana regenerates at 10 points per full hour of uninterrupted rest. Sleep counts as rest. A wounded caster does not regenerate mana at all until the wound has been treated or has healed. A caster who is walking, riding or working regenerates nothing.

### Mana-blindness

About one person in forty is born mana-blind. A mana-blind person has no pool, cannot cast, and cannot be healed or hidden by magic, but they are also immune to scrying and to charms. Mana-blindness is permanent. It cannot be cured, and nobody has ever acquired a pool later in life.

## The Three Disciplines

There are three disciplines of magic. Fire, also called pyromancy, creates and shapes flame and heat. Frost, also called cryomancy, creates and shapes ice and cold. Aether covers everything that works on distance and boundaries: teleportation, wards and scrying.

A minor spell of any discipline costs between 5 and 15 mana. A fire bolt costs 12 mana and a frost lance costs 15 mana. Greater workings, such as summoning a storm, cost far more and are limited to Archmages.

## Casting Rules

### Fire magic and the Ice Citadel

Fire magic cannot be cast inside the Ice Citadel unless the caster possesses flint. The walls of the Citadel are woven with the Frost Binding, an old working that smothers every spell of the Fire discipline the moment it is cast. Flint is the single exception, because a flint holds a stolen spark that the Frost Binding recognises and lets pass.

Possession means carrying the flint on one's person. It does not have to be struck, held in the hand or shown to anyone. A caster with a flint in a pocket can cast fire magic anywhere inside the Citadel.

The ban applies to magic only. Torches, candles, forge fires and the Great Hearth burn normally inside the Citadel, because ordinary fire is not a spell.

### Teleportation

Teleportation costs 50 mana and cannot cross warded walls. The caster names a destination within one league (about five kilometres) and arrives there at once. A caster can carry only what they can carry on their own body. A teleporting caster cannot take another person with them.

Teleportation fails if the straight line to the destination crosses a warded wall, in either direction. A caster can teleport out of a warded place only if the path out does not cross a ward, which in practice almost never happens.

### Wards

A ward is a working of the Aether discipline that is anchored to a wall or a threshold. A warded wall blocks teleportation and scrying in both directions. A ward stays in place until it is broken or until it expires, and most wards are renewed every year by the Aether-mages who keep them.

Only a seal of the Frost Wardens can lower a ward, and only for one hour at a time. The Warden-General alone holds the seal of the Ice Citadel.

### Healing

Healing costs 25 mana for each wound closed. A healer cannot heal themselves, and healing does not restore the mana of the person who is healed. A mana-blind person can be healed, because healing works on the healer's mana and not on the patient's.

### Blood-casting

A caster who is short of mana can pay the missing mana with their own life. Each missing point of mana costs two hit points. This is called blood-casting. It works, but it is forbidden by the Concord of Glass, and a caster who is found to have blood-cast can be stripped of their rank by the Archivists.

## Old Figures

Many old books give the price of teleportation as 30 mana. That was the price before the Sundering, when mana lay close to the surface of the world. The figure of 30 mana is out of date. The price today is 50 mana.
