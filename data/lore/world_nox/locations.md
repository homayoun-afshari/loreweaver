# Places of Nox

## Salt Harbour

Salt Harbour is the great port of Nox, a city of white stone and tall masts on a bay of green water. About twelve thousand people live there. It is ruled by the Harbour Council, a body of nine ship-captains, and it is the only place in Nox where Tide-keys are issued.

### The Tide Wards

The harbour walls are warded. The wards are called the Tide Wards, and they block teleportation in either direction. They are lowered for one hour at every full moon so that ships' crews can teleport their cargo ashore. During that hour a caster can teleport across the harbour walls for the normal price of 80 mana, without a Tide-key.

### The Council House

The Council House stands on the quay. It is the only building in Nox that keeps a register of every Tide-key. A lost Tide-key must be reported within a day, and a replacement costs a month's wages.

## The Hollow Mine

The Hollow Mine lies under the Grey Hills, a day's walk from the coast. It is a deep silver mine that has been worked for three hundred years. About four hundred miners work in it.

### Fire in the Hollow Mine

Fire magic of any kind is forbidden inside the Hollow Mine, because the air holds firedamp. Open flames are forbidden too. Miners use sealed lanterns, which are lit with a spark from outside the mine. A caster who casts a fire spell inside the mine is expelled and fined.

### The Deep Guild

The Deep Guild runs the mine. Its members wear cracked masks of grey clay as a mark of rank, and a mask is cracked deliberately when a miner passes their first year underground. The Guild also keeps the only map of the lower galleries.

## Travel

The road from Salt Harbour to the Hollow Mine takes one day on foot. The Grey Hills are crossed by a single pass that is open all year.
