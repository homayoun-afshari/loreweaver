# Notable Characters of Nox

This document records the backgrounds and traits of the characters that the author has written so far. It describes who they are, not where they are or what they carry at any moment.

## Kael Draven

Kael Draven grew up in the Hollow Mine, the son of two miners, and joined the Deep Guild at fourteen. He is a Journeyman, with a mana pool of up to 150 mana, and the only caster in the mine who is allowed to carry a sealed lantern into the lower galleries. He is quiet, watchful and slow to trust.

Kael is the keeper of the Guild's map of the lower galleries. He has refused twice to copy it for the Harbour Council, and the Council has not forgiven him.

## Mira Sol

Mira Sol is a navigator from Salt Harbour who learned to read the stars from her grandmother. She is a Tidemaster, with a mana pool of up to 300 mana, and one of the few in Nox who can teleport between ships at sea. She is bold, talkative and in debt.

Mira owes the Harbour Council the price of a lost Tide-key. She says that it was stolen, and the Council says that it was sold.
