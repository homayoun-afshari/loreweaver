# The Magic of Nox

Nox is a world of tides and tunnels. Its magic is paid for in mana, like all magic, but the prices are higher than anywhere else and the rules are older. This document lists the general laws.

## Mana

### Pool sizes

Casters in Nox are ranked as Novices, Journeymen and Tidemasters. A Novice holds up to 60 mana, a Journeyman holds up to 150 mana, and a Tidemaster holds up to 300 mana.

### Regeneration

Mana regenerates at 20 points per full hour of rest, whether the caster is wounded or not. Mana regenerates twice as fast on a ship at sea, at 40 points per hour, because the tides feed it.

## Casting Rules

### Teleportation

Teleportation costs 80 mana in Nox. It can cross a warded wall only if the caster carries a Tide-key, a small disc of grey shell that is issued by the Harbour Council. Without a Tide-key, teleportation cannot cross a warded wall.

### Fire magic

Fire magic can be cast anywhere in Nox without any special object, with one exception: it is forbidden inside the Hollow Mine, where the air holds firedamp that burns. Flint has no meaning for magic in Nox.

### Healing

Healing costs 40 mana for each wound closed. A healer can heal themselves, but only at double cost.

### Starlight

A caster who is under an open night sky can draw on starlight to cast one minor spell without paying mana. This works once per night, and it works only for spells that cost 15 mana or less.
