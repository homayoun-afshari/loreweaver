# The Moon Archive

## Overview

The Moon Archive is a small library cut into the cliffs above the Silver Bay. It keeps the tide tables and the star charts of the coast. About thirty scribes work there, and they are proud of the lamps that burn in every reading room.

## Visiting hours

Visitors may enter the Moon Archive at dusk and must leave before the second bell of night. The Door of Tides, on the seaward side, is the only public entrance.
